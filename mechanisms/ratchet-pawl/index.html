Ratchet and pawl

A lever that swings both ways, driving a wheel that only turns one way.

0°

Each tooth is a one-way door: a steep face to push against and a long slope for the pawl to climb. One pawl drives, a second one holds, and the wheel advances one tooth per stroke.
