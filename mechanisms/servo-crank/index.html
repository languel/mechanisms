Servo crank

A servo holds an angle, not a speed. Half a crank circle is all it can give you — and that decides the whole motion.

0°

A servo crank is half a crank: the slider goes from the middle of its stroke to the end and back, so pick the arm radius for the stroke and the rod for the smoothness you can afford.
