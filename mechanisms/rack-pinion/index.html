Rack and pinion

A gear unrolled into a straight line: every radian of the pinion is one pitch radius of travel.

0°

The rack is the pinion’s pitch circle unrolled. Match the module and the tooth spacing lines up; the travel per turn is fixed by the pitch radius, not by the teeth you can see.
